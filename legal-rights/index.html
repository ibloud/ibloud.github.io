Use cases within their limits

Legal interpretation, professional review pending: Childress examines joint-author intent; Aalmuhammed shows that valuable creative assistance does not itself establish coauthorship. Effects Associates distinguishes ownership transfer from an implied nonexclusive license on its facts. Thaler addresses a machine designated sole author, not every mixed human/AI workflow. Jurisdiction and facts matter; no universal joint-author test or current litigation-finality claim is made. Use the original decisions and qualified review before applying them.

What each evidence label means

  • Verified legal authority: retrieved statute, judicial text or agency guidance with jurisdiction/version/date and host limitations. Agency guidance and binding law still differ.
  • Verified project implementation: inspected source or observed behavior with an exact commit/test; not blanket live acceptance.
  • User-supplied claim: attributed observation not independently repeated.
  • Proposed implementation: a design or possible next action, not an enabled capability.
  • Legal interpretation: provisional application to stated facts, requiring qualified review.
  • Unresolved fact: unknown evidence stays unknown.
  • Professional review pending: no professional approval is asserted. Synthetic sample records carry a separate fictional example label.
