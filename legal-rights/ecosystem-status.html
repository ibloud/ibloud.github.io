It contains no verified formation instrument, state file number, current entity record, operating agreement, bylaws, tax election, tax-exempt determination or entity-level IP assignment. This is a statement about the documents reviewed—not a conclusion from a completed government registry search that no entity exists.

  • Each project's own repository and signed agreements remain the relevant local authority. Shared ecosystem presentation does not create a blanket assignment, license, partnership, joint venture, employer relationship or common ownership arrangement.

3. Business form and relationship questions remain open

The current mission statement's “pre-seed venture” description is an owner-approved business-stage statement, not a legal filing. The reviewed records do not select between sole proprietorship, LLC, corporation, cooperative, nonprofit or another structure. No entity-level parent/subsidiary, holding-company, joint-venture, franchise, partnership or employer relationship should be inferred from the ecosystem map, repository organization, shared branding, domains, or project dependencies.

Before a public offer, contract, invoice, grant, investment, paid commission, worker engagement, royalty administration or transfer of intellectual property, the actual contracting party and rights holder must be identified in writing. Record who signs, who receives funds, who performs the work, who owns or licenses each asset, who controls data, and who bears obligations. A future structure decision must be reviewed for tax, employment, IP, insurance, privacy, benefits and any applicable housing-program implications before implementation.

Current participation terms say open review and contribution opportunities are voluntary and unpaid; participation alone does not promise employment, ownership, revenue share, academic credit or future pay. Any paid commission or other formal arrangement requires a separate signed agreement before work begins. Do not convert that policy into an implied employment or ownership arrangement.

Owner-designated public support destinations are Buy Me a Coffee, Patreon, GitHub Sponsors and PayPal.me/ibloud. Each platform controls its own tier names, amounts, fees and terms. These are optional ways to support Loptr Lab's work, not a single Mayhem membership ladder, not proof of a legal entity, and not evidence of any particular revenue amount.

The public repository materials reviewed for this update do not include platform payout statements, gross-to-net reconciliations, refunds or chargebacks, payment fees, bank deposits, accounting ledgers, entity-level financial statements, audited accounts, tax returns or information returns. Private provider dashboards, bank records, contracts and tax documents were not inspected. This is a limit on the evidence reviewed; it does not establish that private bookkeeping, filings or a legal entity do not exist.

The legal account holder and recipient for each channel, the relevant taxpayer, any applicable entity classification, and the tax treatment of the receipts are not established by the public brand or repository names. “PIXIE Holdings” is a project/repository/product label, not proof of a formed holding company. Do not describe support as charitable or tax-deductible, issue an entity invoice or receipt, or present consolidated financials until the actual recipient, legal status and records are verified.

Tax reporting depends on the actual business structure and recipient. The IRS explains that a sole proprietorship is an unincorporated business with no legal identity apart from its owner; this register does not determine that Loptr Lab or any project is a sole proprietorship. See IRS Topic 407: Business income, IRS Publication 583: Starting a Business and Keeping Records, and IRS recordkeeping guidance. Obtain qualified tax/accounting advice after confirming the platform account owner, legal payee, payout records and applicable business form.

The Holdings repository maintains the operational funding and entity-status record. The Gods of Mayhem page describes the same open-participation and optional-support boundary. Platform support is distinct from B2B partnerships, licenses, commissions and royalties, which require a verified contracting party and separate written terms.

5. Brand, copyright and project-rights controls

  • Keep trademark rights separate from copyright in text, artwork, software, recordings and other works. A state trademark filing is not copyright registration and does not establish federal trademark clearance.
  • For each name, maintain exact mark/logo version, claimant, evidence of earliest documented use, claimed commercial first use, actual/proposed goods/services, territories, similar-mark search scope, official registry results, application/registration number and current status—only where verified.
  • For each work, keep authorship/contributor records, source files, licenses, assignments, permissions, releases, music composition/master chains, third-party assets and any AI-assisted contribution disclosures separately attributable.
  • Do not infer a transfer or blanket license from a GitHub organization, repository membership, public collaboration, a pull request, credit, shared portfolio, shared domain or participation in the ecosystem.
  • Use each repository's exact license and canon authority. Known license conflicts or unresolved provenance remain unresolved until the owner and qualified reviewer document a resolution.

7. Status vocabulary used across the ecosystem

  • Documented: found in an identified repository, public page or owner-approved statement; only proves the documented fact.
  • Owner-confirmed direction: an attributed project/business intention; not legal formation or a transfer.
  • Proposed: an option or intended future arrangement, not implemented or binding.
  • Implemented: present in source code or a repository artifact; does not itself establish production use, ownership or compliance.
  • Registry-verified: official record retrieved and identified by jurisdiction, query, file/serial number, date and status.
  • Contract-verified: relevant signed instrument inspected; scope, parties, date and governing terms recorded privately where needed.
  • Unverified / unknown: no adequate evidence in the reviewed record. Do not convert absence of evidence into a favorable finding.
  • Professional review pending: no legal approval, clearance or individualized advice is asserted.
