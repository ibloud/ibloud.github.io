About / 03

A practice built from music, strategy, games and systems thinking.

I am Dominique Devereaux, a founder and lead systems architect working at the edge of creative direction and dependable implementation. I care about the moment a complicated idea becomes something another person can enter, test and make their own.

Accessibility is part of the architecture, not a final polish pass. My work includes games, research, creator resources and the connective tissue that helps people understand what exists, what is changing and what still needs proof.
