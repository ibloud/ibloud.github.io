Research & Practice

Leading the Build

Organizational Leadership and Narrative World-Building in Independent Game Studio Production

Primary research question

How do leadership and production structures in small, founder-led independent game studios shape — and get shaped by — the narrative worlds they build?

The project investigates studio production as a longitudinal creative practice rather than treating a single game as the research object. It asks whether narrative systems, lore, mechanics, shared language, and world-building principles can become part of the organizational infrastructure through which a small studio understands identity, priorities, collaboration, and direction.

These projects are not presented only as finished portfolio pieces. Their repositories, design decisions, documentation, experiments, and production history form an evolving record of practice through which the research questions can be examined.

PRIMARY CREATIVE CASE

Veiled Dominion

An open prototype exploring strategic mechanics through restraint, regulation, asymmetric systems, and inclusive architecture. The project provides a site for examining how design principles become technical and organizational constraints.

ACCESSIBILITY ARTIFACT

Duet

An accessibility-first browser prototype designed around screen-reader interaction, coordinate commands, visual testing, procedural audio cues, and a snapshot-oriented interface architecture. It treats accessibility as a systems constraint, not merely a presentation layer.
