Meet the creator

Dominique Devereaux

I created Loptr Lab to connect storytelling, accessible technology and practical opportunities for people to learn, create and contribute.

My work began with stories about identity, autonomy and relationships. I now use those questions to guide experimental games, creator resources and an open-development studio pilot. People come first; the technology is a means to make the work possible.

Why I created this ecosystem

Creative work often gets separated from the tools, people and support needed to sustain it. I wanted a connected way to discover reliable resources, find meaningful work and learn through real projects, without taking ownership away from creators.

Loptr Lab is the studio and public record. Made-Sick is the resource directory. Training develops practical skills. PIXIE is the project companion that helps people find context and continue work. Games and stories remain distinct creative projects. Explore how the pieces connect.

How I use AI

I set the creative direction, requirements, priorities and acceptance criteria. AI assists with drafting, research synthesis, coding, troubleshooting, accessibility planning and documentation. I review the outputs and record what works, what fails and what remains unfinished. Generated work is not automatically a finished product.
