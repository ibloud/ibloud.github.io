Account hosting and public data

The @made-sick.org AT Protocol account migrated to Eurosky on 7 October 2026. Its Personal Data Server hosts the account under Eurosky’s applicable rules. This does not relocate the Made Sick website or establish compliance for every Loptr Lab project. Participant records belong to each participant’s chosen PDS.
