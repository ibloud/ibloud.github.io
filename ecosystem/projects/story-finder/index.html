AT Protocol features
Resolves handles to DIDs with com.atproto.identity.resolveHandle and reads app.bsky.feed.getAuthorFeed. Local word, phrase and date filters and selected-reference ledger export are implemented. Live check on 7 October 2026: 101 public posts loaded; PIXIE search returned nine matches. Export is local; publishing is not connected.

Account hosting and public data

Made Sick’s @made-sick.org account is hosted on Eurosky. Story Finder reads public feeds through Bluesky’s AppView; it does not log in to or write to that PDS. Account hosting does not mean this tool’s retrieval or exported public references stay within the EU.

Made Sick account hosting: evidence and policy baseline. Public AT Protocol records may be indexed outside the EU. Each application has its own rules and privacy practices.
