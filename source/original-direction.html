Current direction · design foundation

Build an independent world.

New characters start with their own motivations, relationships, histories and visual identities. They are not renamed Paragon heroes. Original writing and generic placeholders support the first prototype; future art, voices and other assets need documented provenance and permitted uses.

Hero / Major Arcana

An archetype frames a character tension and signature play hypothesis. It does not assign a numerical power rank or diagnose a player.

Build / Minor Arcana

Modifiers offer tactical choices with explicit costs, cooldowns, limits and counters. Symbolic numbers do not establish damage or balance.

Story / Spread

Authored situations, pressures and turning points respond to choices. A draw offers an opportunity; the player determines the action.

First playable milestone

One original hero, one modifier and one consequential authored choice. Replay must reproduce the selected scene and consequences; replaying a choice must not apply its consequences twice. A changed choice must change a later objective or ending condition.

Start with plain-language paths, text suit labels, readable contrast, large touch targets and optional reduced motion. These are prototype acceptance requirements, not completed accessibility checks.

Tactical language to test

Swords: precision and information, with timing and exposure costs. Staves: movement and burst, with commitment and recovery. Cups: protection and support, constrained by range and shared resources. Coins: preparation and reserves, with setup delay.

Balance comes from explicit rules, exploit checks and human playtesting. Optional upright/reversed approaches describe tradeoffs, not automatic good/evil or stronger/weaker states. Cardology adapters remain deferred; a playing-card pack is not automatically equivalent to a tarot deck.

This Codex entry indexes the proposal; it does not define game rules. Canonical Veiled Dominion, Duet, PIXIE’s reflection deck and unrelated bots retain their existing designs.
