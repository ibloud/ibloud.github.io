# Shared ecosystem instructions

Scope: shared contribution principles for Loptr Lab and ibloud projects. Recorded
16 November 2026 under owner direction; reviewed through existing portfolio PR #59.
This file lives in the existing ecosystem documentation home. It is not a new
product, canon authority, governance body, integration or release permission.

## Read and establish authority

Read the current task, this document, the owning repository's root and applicable
nested `AGENTS.md`, its source/configuration and relevant open issues/PRs before
acting. A link does not automatically load or inherit instructions; neither MCP
nor GitHub is claimed to enforce them. Use a local portfolio checkout or explicitly
retrieve the exact shared source linked by the repository. If unavailable, name
that limitation and stop only the dependent work; never invent its contents.

Direct owner instructions govern the authorized task. Keep project-specific canon,
security, privacy, licensing, tests and release authority in the owning repository.
Do not invent precedence between conflicting records: name both exact sources,
preserve current behavior and request only the decision needed for dependent work.
No shared summary silently changes an existing policy, license or release hold.

## Mission and creative family

Read [MISSION.md](../MISSION.md) for people-over-profit, accessibility-first work,
participation, compensation, attribution and safe-exit terms. Funding sustains the
work; it does not buy membership, canon authority, access or employment.

Owner-approved foundation: **The Diary of Death’s Daughter manga**—the matriarch,
her story and mythology—is Loptr Lab's creative foundation. Projects form a
creative family while retaining their personalities. Gods of Mayhem expresses
that family and retains its established human team/community role. This records
lineage, not a promotion of proposed fiction or rules into another project's canon.
Shared lineage does not establish shared mechanics, authentication, infrastructure,
dependencies or a combined runtime. See the existing
[ecosystem architecture and repository map](ECOSYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md).

Studio art direction favors expressive stylized fantasy, readable silhouettes,
deliberate value hierarchy, culturally specific character detail, controlled color
and purposeful motion. Riot's art education is an external principles reference,
not studio branding or a requirement that all projects look alike. Preserve local
palettes, approved symbols, artwork and stylesheet ownership. Rebirth's anatomical
left eye is crimson red and anatomical right eye gold. Preserve the
[scoped artwork and halo instructions](../ecosystem/projects/deaths-daughter/AGENTS.md);
these instructions do not authorize generating or repainting art.

## Continuity, source verification and scope

Start with current owning sources and unpublished work. Read substantive contents
of any page, file, video or source on which an answer depends; a title, search
snippet, link or dated status snapshot is insufficient. Label unretrieved material
as unretrieved, proposed work as proposed, and uncertainty precisely. Keep original
dates, authorship, identifiers and project boundaries attached to evidence.

Inspect open assignments and PRs before creating work. Reuse or extend overlapping
assignments; do not restart completed work or create parallel directories, engines,
guides or services. Identify the actual gap and smallest compatible change. Record
ideas as proposals; only an explicit scoped decision makes them assignments.

## Accessibility, privacy and honest reporting

Keep keyboard access, screen-reader semantics, contrast, reflow, reduced motion,
consent and recovery behavior intact. State actual browser/device/input checks;
automated Chromium or emulation is not physical iPad/Safari/VoiceOver acceptance.

Minimise data. Keep credentials, private correspondence, sensitive participant
records, health information and unpublished work out of public contribution records.
Discovery, identity, authentication, consent, publication and enrollment are
separate actions. No inferred partnership, diagnosis, private access, credential
verification or rights clearance. Participant attribution and public visibility
require agreement; existing local privacy/security rules remain binding.

Report what was proposed, edited, committed, tested, pushed, merged, previewed,
deployed and observed separately, with exact source and check results. Passing CI,
a reachable page or a merged PR does not prove end-to-end behavior or human acceptance.
No merge, deployment, hosting/DNS change, release or hold removal follows from this
structure. The portfolio's GitHub Pages production/Vercel review arrangement applies
only to the portfolio unless another repository's own configuration establishes it.

## Use what we have first — owner correction, 24 November 2026

This is a mandatory decision gate within the existing order of operations, not a
new workflow or service. Before recommending or implementing automation, asset
generation, integrations, purchases or custom code:

1. Check the current application's documented native capabilities and existing
   settings, including rules, filters, import/export and accessibility features.
   When a native feature meets the need, recommend or use it first within the
   owner's authorization. Explain the shortest usable path, including any device
   or permission requirements.
2. Reuse approved assets, existing files, existing subscriptions/credits and
   established integrations, infrastructure and assignments before generating
   replacements or introducing dependencies. Preserve identity, provenance,
   quality and local authority.
3. Identify the specific gap left by native functionality or existing resources.
   Only then propose a custom automation, integration or new implementation.
   Explain why it is necessary, its maintenance burden, and whether the owner
   can complete the task directly.
4. Before incurring additional cost, obtain explicit approval for the amount
   and purpose. Do not spend credits, purchase tools, start paid services or
   silently duplicate work because a tool is available.

Record the native feature or existing resource checked, the remaining gap (if
any), and the selected action. An assistant-managed workaround is not the
default when the application already solves the problem. If native support
cannot be verified, state that uncertainty; do not assume it does not exist.
Do not claim that repository instructions automatically control another chat,
host, installed plugin or application.

## Required order of operations — owner correction, 16 November 2026

1. Read supplied briefs and affected substantive sources before deciding. Record
   exactly what was read and what remains unavailable; never claim an unread source
   was checked. Search relevant local work, review branches and prior assignments
   before asking the owner to resend existing information.
2. Identify overlapping work and the smallest correction. Extend the existing
   assignment; do not build a replacement and force it to fit.
3. Preserve explicit requirements, including ethnicity, cultural identity, anatomical
   details and approved artwork. Do not substitute defaults or infer missing approval.
   Ask one focused question when a consequential decision is missing or contradictory.
   Apply this standard to incomplete owner directions without making the owner repeat
   directions already supplied.
4. Follow existing code structure, dependencies and stylesheet ownership. Verify the
   requested behavior and supplied acceptance criteria before claiming completion.
   An unresolved requirement or inaccessible required source blocks dependent work.
5. Report evidence, assumptions, changes, tests and remaining gaps separately.
   A task contract is editable reported context, not proof that a source was read.

Legal and rights information is educational, not professional advice or a determination
of ownership, clearance, licensing, compliance or eligibility. Preserve source, date,
asset, use and jurisdiction limits. Refer unresolved professional determinations to an
appropriately qualified professional. Do not present AI assistance as independent review.
