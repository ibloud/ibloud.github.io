# Loptr Lab — public-benefit live/work redevelopment research

**Public feasibility and funding register · 7 November 2026 · Proposed, not funded**

## Purpose

Loptr Lab is exploring an accessible live/work/learn/play model that links affordable housing, independent creative production, technical training and community participation. The aim is to publish reusable requirements, transparent evidence, failed assumptions and practical methods so **qualified organizations and communities can act even if Loptr Lab never purchases or operates a site**.

The immediate [Violet’s Revenge development proposal](../ecosystem/projects/violets-revenge/) remains a bounded game-production pilot. This possible future facility is not part of that pilot’s budget, and no property, government funding or partner has been secured. The [existing mission](../MISSION.md) establishes people-over-profit, accessibility first and basic income as a long-term social goal, not an active payment program.

## Funding corrections and applicant boundaries

- The prior Minneapolis grant strategy named Propel Nonprofits as a prospective fiscal sponsor. [Propel's fiscal sponsorship program was sunset March 31, 2026](https://propelnonprofits.org/blog/programmatic-update-sunsetting-fiscal-sponsorship-at-propel/); do not treat it as available. Any other sponsor must be checked for present eligibility, capacity and permitted grant purposes.
- Historical grant amounts, application success forecasts and administrative-fee estimates in earlier planning are **unverified assumptions**, not current offers or secured funds.
- The studio’s preliminary Phase 2 estimate is **not** a total for land purchase, affordable housing development or environmental remediation. Prepare a project-specific capital and operating budget only after a site and qualified partners are established.
- Personal housing assistance, VRS self-employment support, nonprofit/arts grants, public redevelopment capital, and federal property conveyances each have separate recipients, permitted uses and reporting requirements. They must not be conflated.

## Reproducible feasibility checklist

For each prospective property, publish only cleared information and evaluate: public owner and current status; title/acquisition route; qualified applicant and partner; residential, assembly and commercial zoning; accessibility and independent living; transport and healthcare; building and fire compliance; contamination and rehabilitation; studio utilities and acoustic needs; land and construction costs; operating model; resident protection and long-term affordability; and funding eligibility.

Mark each finding **verified / proposed / unknown** with an official source, date and a go/revise/stop decision. A documented no-go still serves others.

## Economic-security context

Scott Santens's [World Economic Forum opinion article on basic income](https://www.weforum.org/stories/jobs-and-the-future-of-work/why-we-should-all-have-a-basic-income) argues that a secure income floor can support agency and creativity. It is advocacy context, not a government grant, WEF-endorsed project financing, or evidence of a currently available universal-basic-income benefit.

**Privacy and governance:** No Social Security, disability, medical, housing, banking or other private records belong in the public repository. No promises of site ownership, funding, partnership or housing placement without independent verification.
