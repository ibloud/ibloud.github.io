# Loptr Lab Creative Ecosystem Architecture

## Purpose

This document is the canonical public map of how Dominique Devereaux's creative archives, prototypes, games, research, and contributor pathways relate. It records development lineage without declaring every work part of one literal fictional continuity.

## Studio viability pilot — owner-confirmed direction, 18 October 2026

**The Loptr Lab ecosystem is the testing ground for the viability of an open-source multimedia training studio. PIXIE is its contributor companion: helping people join, learn, coordinate work, and return with context intact.** Dominique confirmed this direction on 18 October 2026 and requested that it be maintained in repository documentation rather than depend on assistant memory.

Existing games, stories, media projects, creator tools and training pathways provide real work through which to test the studio model. Their individual repositories retain implementation, license and canon authority. PIXIE provides continuity and contextual guidance across those surfaces; she is not limited to an evidence-audit role or owned by a single game.

### What the pilot must establish

| Question | Evidence to record |
|---|---|
| Can contributors find suitable work and get started? | A reproducible entry path, clear task instructions, observed barriers and an actual first contribution. |
| Does training lead to useful creative and technical work? | The learning activity, resulting artifact, review and what the contributor could do afterward. |
| Can people resume and collaborate with less repeated explanation? | Shared checkpoints, decisions, blockers and handoffs; examples of context retained or missed and avoidable rework. |
| Can the studio sustain itself financially? | Candidate customers and offers, delivery/maintenance costs, feedback and evidence of willingness to pay. Treat revenue assumptions as hypotheses until tested. |

Use participant-approved observations and existing project records. Do not introduce private profiling or unnecessary data collection to measure the pilot. Record what failed as well as what worked; do not invent targets, validated demand, revenue or acceptance.

### PIXIE testing and release priorities

The immediate purpose is usable contributor testing within the existing ecosystem while PIXIE is still in development. Mark unfinished capabilities, provide working participation paths and capture defects and progress in GitHub. A complete commercial product is not a prerequisite for this testing.

Contributor access to the website, installing a development/test plugin connection, public-directory approval and commercial readiness are separate milestones. Verify each actual access path with another contributor; never describe a private plugin release or public website as proof that others can install the ChatGPT plugin.

Clippy is a reference for contextual assistance; Atmosfear is a reference for recurring presence and dramatic timing. These are design references, not authorization to import their characters, assets or game rules. Keep contributor guidance accessible and user-controlled.

### Mandatory existing-work check — owner direction, 23 October 2026

Before proposing or creating a directory, database, guide, repository, plugin, page or workflow:

1. Inspect available local files and existing project instructions first.
2. Read this canonical map and the affected project's current source. Use connected GitHub and existing PIXIE tools when local source is absent; a failed retrieval is not evidence that the work does not exist.
3. Identify the existing implementation or resource, its owning source and the precise unmet need. Reuse, link or extend it. Do not create a parallel solution merely because it is easier to start fresh.
4. If source cannot be retrieved, report the specific gap and continue source discovery; do not claim absence or propose a replacement as established necessity.
5. Before any new artifact, state what was checked, what will be reused and why an addition is necessary. Keep that evidence concise in the work record.

Public discovery and learning how to connect can use the established surfaces. Private briefs, correspondence and follow-up records must not be placed in public contribution records. A new professional database is not the default; establish a specific unmet need before proposing one.

## Development lineage

The working lineage is:

1. Lady Violet and Sebastian establish the political grammar.
2. *The RIM* develops the distributed method of story discovery.
3. Urban Alien Adventures places narrative fragments across apparently separate media.
4. *Battle the Beast* prototypes nonlinear navigation, recurrent identities, and ensemble discovery.
5. *Violet's Revenge* develops the inherited investigative branch.
6. *Veiled Dominion* expresses transferable principles through original systems and mechanics.
7. Loptr Lab research and training examine how that world-building shapes production and collaboration.

## Transfer rule

Transfer design questions, principles, and testable player experiences only. Do not transfer franchise characters, protected expression, recordings, lyrics, images, or other third-party material into original commercial IP. A concept becomes canon only through the authority process of its destination repository.

## Discovery architecture

Urban Alien Adventures is not merely a collection title. It is a delivery model:

1. The audience encounters advertisements, artifacts, broadcasts, or background details.
2. Recurring signals reveal that apparently separate pieces belong together.
3. The audience identifies the players and their relationships.
4. The promotional or environmental layer is recognized as part of the narrative.
5. Participation changes from passive viewing to investigation and play.

John August's film [*The Nines*](https://johnaugust.com/projects/the-movie) is identified as a structural inspiration for layered identity and reality discovery. Inspiration does not imply affiliation, license, adaptation rights, or ownership.

## Canon rule

When documents conflict, follow the canon policy in the repository responsible for the work. This architecture is a map, not a mechanism for overriding local canon.
