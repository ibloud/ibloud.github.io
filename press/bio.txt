Dominique Devereaux, also known as I.B. Loud, is the Founder & Lead Systems Architect of Loptr Lab. Her academic background spans government administration, network and communication management, and organizational leadership. She holds an MPA, MBA and BS in Management and previously pursued doctoral coursework in Organizational Leadership – Information Technology at the University of Phoenix and continues her research independently. Her work examines institutional accountability, equitable access, technology ethics and human-centered systems design. Her scholarly record includes a 2007 study of institutional sexual assault and harassment policies.
