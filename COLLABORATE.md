# Collaborate with Dominique Devereaux and Loptr Lab

Start with the [creative ecosystem map](https://ibloud.github.io/ecosystem/) and the [public collaboration pathways](https://ibloud.github.io/collaborate/).

## Current areas of need

- Narrative continuity and distributed-story design
- Archival inventory and provenance verification
- Ink and accessible web development
- Game systems and structured playtesting
- Accessibility review across sensory and cognitive needs
- Production planning, budgeting, and partnerships
- IP, music, asset, and contributor-rights review
- Practice-research and academic collaboration

## Propose a contribution

Email `questions@loptrlab.com` with:

1. The project and narrowly defined problem you can help address
2. A relevant portfolio, repository, publication, or experience
3. Your availability and proposed deliverable
4. Whether you require compensation, academic approval, or another formal arrangement
5. Any access needs relevant to the collaboration process

Do not send confidential, medical, benefits, housing, banking, or identity records in an initial inquiry.

## Participation boundary

A conversation, issue, fork, or pull request is not an offer of employment, academic credit, compensation, revenue share, ownership, access, or canon authority. Before substantive work begins, the parties should identify in writing:

- scope and deliverables;
- participation status: paid, funded research, academic, volunteer, exploratory, or awaiting funding;
- credit and portfolio rights;
- ownership and license treatment;
- confidentiality and data access;
- review and canon authority; and
- termination or handoff conditions.

Repository-specific contribution and license terms continue to apply.
