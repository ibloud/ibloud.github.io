# Phuture Me — project fit review

Reviewed October 8, 2026 (America/Chicago). Public pages only; no account, paid feature, device interaction or import/export trial performed.

## Recommendation

Use as a design reference and candidate for a small manual authoring trial. Do not adopt it as the game engine, balance authority, health system or source of cleared assets.

| Project | Useful application | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Return to the Void | Compare symbolic tensions and spread positions; prototype situation / pressure / turning point with original text | Our authored choices and rules determine consequences; numerology does not set damage, costs or win rates |
| PIXIE Reflection | Explore user-selected cards, optional guidance and saved reflection notes | Keep the custom 54-card structure and voluntary prompts; do not infer health, destiny or personal traits |
| Codex | Study deck discovery, clear card meanings and user control over interpretation | Retain our own design, content and provenance |
| Veiled Dominion / Sealed Deck | Research reference for narrative presentation | No change to canonical rules, legacy card effects, suits or existing bots |
| Made Sick / health-facing PIXIE | Little value beyond fictional workshop prompts | Not a clinical assessment, treatment or personal prediction service |

## Verified offerings and limits

The app advertises Pro custom deck/spread tools, saved-reading search/export, and iOS availability. The FAQ documents `.spread` and `.spreads` files. Those features do not establish compatibility with our JSON, complete backup fidelity or accessible iPad operation. Optional purchases exist; no purchase is recommended until a small import/export trial proves useful.

AI interpretations combine a question with cards and spread positions. The service describes a single interpretation rather than follow-up dialogue. The privacy policy says AI reading content is sent to third-party processors; local saved content can remain on-device, with online features and backups changing where it goes. Keep trial content fictional and omit private user or health data.

The terms describe adult entertainment services and restrict reproduction of site materials. Their deck library is not a source of reusable licensed artwork for our project. No public developer API was verified in the reviewed material. This is a research recommendation, not a legal clearance or integration decision.

## Proposed bounded trial

1. Use original placeholder cards representing situation, pressure and turning point; no historical Paragon identities or copied imagery.
2. Check whether Pro import preserves our identifiers, text and card ordering; verify whether PIXIE's 54-card structure is supported.
3. Try selection, optional reversal labels and reading notes on iPad with large text and VoiceOver. Record failures rather than assuming accessibility.
4. Export and reopen a sample. Keep our canonical source outside the app; verify which images, notes and IDs survive.
5. Compare two player choices using our own authored encounter rules. Require a different later objective or ending condition, not merely different narration.
6. Stop at a manual design trial unless permissions, data portability and an explicit integration contract justify further work.
