Collaboration pathways

Help make the architecture playable.

Loptr Lab is a pre-seed, people-over-profit, accessibility-first venture working toward a self-sustaining model within a capitalist economy. Money sustains the work; meaningful change for people is its purpose. We accept funding only on terms that keep people and accessibility first. Our long-term vision includes universal basic income. We aim to bring change to life and leave a transparent record of what we tried, what worked, and what failed so others can carry it forward. This mission governs our projects, funding decisions, and partnerships; it is not a temporary marketing position.

Current open review and contribution opportunities are voluntary and unpaid. Before work begins, agree in writing on scope, time, what will be public, credit preferences, and an exit path. You can stop at any point. Participation does not promise employment, ownership, revenue share, academic credit, or future pay. Any paid commission or other formal arrangement requires a separate signed agreement before work begins. External assistance or benefits belong to the participant and are not compensation from Loptr Lab.

Read the full mission and participation terms

The ecosystem needs collaborators who can preserve its history, challenge its assumptions, and translate its design principles into experiences people can use. Participation begins with a scoped conversation and written terms.

  1. Read the ecosystem map.
  2. Choose one defined project or evidence problem.
  3. Share a relevant portfolio, repository, publication, or lived-experience perspective.
  4. Agree in writing on scope, access, credit, rights, compensation status, and review authority before work begins.

Participation status

A public repository, conversation, issue, or pull request is not by itself an offer of employment, academic credit, compensation, revenue share, ownership, or canon authority. Each requires explicit written agreement. Opportunities must be labeled paid under a signed agreement, funded research, academic with institutional approval, voluntary, or exploratory. Future funding or pay is not promised.

Propose a focused contribution

For an open contribution, start with Training, choose a project and prepare a checkpoint through PIXIE. Submit it using your own GitHub account and agree scope with the maintainer. For a private commission or institutional discussion, email the project, scope, availability and compensation requirements. Do not send confidential records in an initial message.
