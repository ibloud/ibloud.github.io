A shared workshop surface for reviewing two distinct games. Each system keeps its own rules, source material, prototype status and review records.

Source and privacy boundary

These workspaces do not merge game state, card definitions, mechanics or bots. Review notes stay in this browser until exported or cleared. Nothing is uploaded, synchronized or sent to a bot.

Canonical game system

Veiled Dominion

The current four-player, 14×14 cross-board game. Its canonical rules and engine live in the Veiled Dominion Engine repository.

Engine under development

This workspace is for rules and engine review. It does not claim a complete playable match engine and does not invent missing movement, combat or terminal rules.
