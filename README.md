> Independent game development as creative and organizational practice.

This repository is the public portfolio and development workspace for **Dominique Devereaux** and **Loptr Lab**.

## Research & Practice

### Leading the Build

**Organizational Leadership and Narrative World-Building in Independent Game Studio Production**

**Primary research question:**

> How do leadership and production structures in small, founder-led independent game studios shape — and get shaped by — the narrative worlds they build?

The proposed practice-research programme uses **Loptr Lab as its primary longitudinal practice site**.

The research investigates the relationship between:

- founder-led leadership;
- production structures;
- narrative and world-building;
- game systems and mechanics;
- accessibility;
- knowledge transfer; and
- the changing conditions of independent production.

One proposition under investigation is:

> **The game world can function as organizational infrastructure.**

Lore can become shared vocabulary. Factions can become conceptual models. Mission structures can encode priorities. Narrative principles can become creative constraints. The identity of a fictional world can become connected to the identity of the studio that builds it.

This repository provides part of the evidence base for investigating those relationships.

## Practice Infrastructure

The projects below are treated as connected parts of an evolving studio practice rather than isolated portfolio pieces.

### Veiled Dominion
**Primary creative case**

[Veiled Dominion Engine](https://github.com/Loptr-Lab/veiled-dominion-engine)

An open prototype for a four-player chess variant organized around restraint rather than conquest.

The project brings together game mechanics, narrative principles, systems architecture, accessibility, and production decisions.

---

### Duet
**Accessibility artifact**

[Duet: Solo](https://github.com/Loptr-Lab/duet-solo-hackathon)

A screen-reader-first accessibility-built chess variant developed for the Build with Gemini XPRIZE.

The project explores accessibility as a systems and architectural constraint rather than something added after the core interaction has been designed.

---

### Made Sick
**Consent-first creator care and economic agency**

[Made Sick repository](https://github.com/ibloud/made-sick)

Made Sick is an independent Loptr Lab prototype for creator-owned stories, meaningful milestones, carefully labeled wellness practices, and voluntary participation. Its PIXIE layer supports care actions chosen by the user, accepts changing capacity, and allows pause or withdrawal without penalty.

The project is Dominique Devereaux’s independent expression of the spirit behind Chris Do’s **1 Billion Mission**: helping people make a living doing what they love. Creators looking for practical education in creative business, branding, pricing, and sustainable careers are referred to [The Futur](https://thefutur.com/).

Made Sick and Loptr Lab are not affiliated with or endorsed by Chris Do or The Futur. The referral is optional and remains separate from PIXIE’s health, consent, and participation features.

## Research Method

The proposed research follows a longitudinal practice-research approach.

### Practice
Ongoing Loptr Lab production provides the primary site of inquiry.

### Archive
Repositories, design documents, production records, version histories, prototypes, pitch materials, and public development writing provide evidence.

### Reflection
Significant creative, technical, leadership, and production decisions are documented and examined reflexively.

### Comparison
Approximately two to three independent or founder-led studios may provide comparative cases, selected using explicit criteria and appropriate public or interview evidence.

### Analysis
Practice and comparative evidence will be considered alongside literature on organizational leadership, creative industries, independent game production, narrative/world-building, accessibility, and practice research.

The working analytical loop is:

**Practice / Artifact → Question → Method → Evidence → Analysis → Finding → Contribution**

---

## Contribution Under Investigation

The research is intended to explore possible contributions across four connected areas:

**Organizational leadership**  
Founder-led leadership under the particular constraints and opportunities of independent game production.

**Creative practice**  
How organizational decisions become embedded in narrative, mechanics, world-building, and systems — and how those creative structures can subsequently influence organizational decisions.

**Practice research**  
How longitudinal independent studio practice can function as a sustained site of doctoral inquiry when creative artifacts are combined with reflexive, comparative, and documentary evidence.

**Industry practice**  
Potential frameworks for independent founders around organizational structure, creative identity, production sustainability, knowledge transfer, and intellectual property.

These are research directions, not predetermined findings.
